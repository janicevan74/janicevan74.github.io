Scale basics

What “1:12” actually means

Scale is a ratio. In 1:12, one inch in your miniature stands for twelve inches, one foot, in real life. Everything shrinks by the same amount, so a room only looks right when every piece in it uses the same scale.

Checking scale before you buy or build

  • Measure one familiar thing. A chair seat should be about 3 in high in 1:6, 1½ in in 1:12, or ¾ in in 1:24. If it isn't, the piece is a different scale.
  • Mixed scales show right away. 1:24 furniture in a 1:12 room looks like children's furniture. 1:12 pieces won't fit in a 1:24 house at all.
  • Same scale, different spelling. Listings may write 1:12, 1/12, 1 12 or “one-inch scale.” They all mean the same thing.
  • Working in metric? Divide the real size in millimeters by 6, 12 or 24. A 900 mm counter becomes 75 mm.
